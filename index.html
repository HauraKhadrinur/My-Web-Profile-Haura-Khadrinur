Areas of Expertise

Software Development Engineering

Web Development

Database Management

Software Testing & Debugging

Pengujian fungsionalitas sistem (Black-box & White-box testing), pelacakan bug, verifikasi kebutuhan, dan evaluasi kualitas perangkat lunak.
